# S1E34 — After 1940: A Connected World

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Since mid-century, deliberate cooperation — UN and beyond, health, science, standards, transport, and digital nets — shrinks the planet into a contested “global village”; rivalry and exclusion never vanish.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 34)  
**Comes from:** S1E33 (~1940 threshold)  
**Goes to:** S1E35 (series coda)

---

## Learning goals

1. Anchor the chapter **after 1940 / WWII**.  
2. Survey **institutions of cooperation** (UN ecosystem overview).  
3. Show **public goods**: health, weather/science data, technical standards.  
4. Trace **shrinking distance**: aviation, satellites, internet.  
5. Hold a **dual lens**: cooperation gains vs. Cold War, inequality, fragility.
