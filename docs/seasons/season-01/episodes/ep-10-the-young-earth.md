**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Earth and Moon are forged in violence; magma oceans give way to crust, air, and water — habitability is earned, not granted — opening the door to life’s chapters.

## Learning goals

1. Describe Earth forming by **accretion** and giant impacts.  
2. Tell the leading **Moon-forming impact** story with ladder honesty.  
3. Outline paths to **atmosphere and oceans** (outgassing + delivery — debated mix).  
4. Introduce **geological deep time** as Earth’s own clock.  
5. Frame **preconditions for life** without jumping to origin mechanisms (Ep. 11–13).

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The collision

- Mars-sized body strikes a proto-Earth; debris disk; Moon assembling.  
- Line: *Our oldest companion may be the scar of a birth wound.*  
- Title: **The Young Earth**

### Act I — Building a planet

**Beat A — Accretion**  
- Planetesimals smash and stick; Earth grows; heat from impacts and radioactivity.  
- Magma ocean phases — glowing world.

**Beat B — Differentiation**  
- Iron sinks to core; rocky mantle/crust begin — **Established** broad picture.  
- Magnetic field tease (shield later important).

**Beat C — Timeline**  
- Master timeline: ~4.5 billion years ago region; Hadean eon named lightly.

### Act II — The Moon

**Beat D — Giant-impact hypothesis**  
- Leading explanation for Moon’s makeup and orbit — **Strong inference** (dominant), still refined (**Active debate** on details: impactor, timing, multiple impacts).  
- Samples from Apollo as evidence anchors.

**Beat E — Why the Moon matters to the story**  
- Tides, night sky, possible climate/obliquity roles — keep cautious, not mystical destiny.

### Act III — Atmosphere and oceans

**Beat F — First atmospheres**  
- Lost and replaced; volcanic outgassing; composition uncertain in detail — **Active debate**.  
- Not today’s oxygen-rich air (oxygen story much later, Ep. 15).

**Beat G — Water**  
- Sources: volcanic vs. asteroids/comets vs. mix — **Active debate**.  
- Visual: steam atmosphere → rain → first oceans.

**Beat H — A surface appears**  
- Crust solidifies in patches; bombardment continues (Late Heavy Bombardment — mention carefully as **debated timing/intensity**).  
- Habitability windows may open surprisingly early — **Active debate**.

### Act IV — Preconditions, honesty, baton

**Beat I — What “ready for life” means here**  
- Energy gradients, liquid water, chemistry ingredients — checklist without claiming life yet.  
- Line: *A stage can be built before the play begins.*

**Beat J — Honesty beat**  
- Known: ancient Earth/Moon system; differentiation; water existed early enough for later life.  
- Unknown: exact water sources; earliest atmosphere recipes; how continuous early habitability was.

**Beat K — Baton**  
- On this restless world, something will leave traces in rock — or will it be unambiguous?  
- Tease **The First Life** (evidence before origin theories).

## Honesty beat (closing card)

**Known:** Violent birth; Moon linked to giant impact story; water and atmosphere emerged in deep time.  
**Unknown:** Exact recipes and timings of early air and oceans.  
**Better evidence:** Ancient zircons and rocks; isotopic fingerprints; comparative planetology.
