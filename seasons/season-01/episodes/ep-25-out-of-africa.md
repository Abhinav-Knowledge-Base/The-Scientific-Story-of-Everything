# S1E25 — Out of Africa

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** *Homo sapiens* arise within Africa and disperse across the globe — reconstructed from fossils, archaeology, and genetics, with honest uncertainty about exact pulses and routes.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 25)  
**Comes from:** S1E24 (human evolutionary tree)  
**Goes to:** S1E26 (evolution of the human mind)

---

## Learning goals

1. State **African origin** of *Homo sapiens* as the leading evidence-based framework.  
2. Trace high-level dispersals to **Eurasia, Australia, the Americas**.  
3. Integrate **fossil, archaeological, and genetic** evidence types.  
4. Keep **timing and routes** as actively refined, not frozen textbook dogma.  
5. Hand off to mind/culture as what travelers carried.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The drawing map

- Africa lights first; arrows pulse outward in waves (not one cartoon arrow).  
- Genetic gradient overlays whisper across continents.  
- Line: *We are an African species that learned the whole planet.*  
- Title: **Out of Africa**

### Act I — Origins

**Beat A — *Homo sapiens* in Africa**  
- Fossils and archaeology; deep African population structure — not a single tiny village myth.  
- Age language: on the order of **~300,000 years** for early *sapiens* (soft edges; **Active debate** on definitions/specimens).

**Beat B — Evidence braid**  
- Bones + tools/ornaments + DNA — consilience and conflicts.

### Act II — Dispersal

**Beat C — Into Eurasia**  
- Corridors and bottlenecks; climate windows.  
- Encounters with Neanderthals/Denisovans — callback Ep. 24 admixture.

**Beat D — Multiple pulses**  
- Avoid single-exodus oversimplification; **Active debate** on early vs. later successful waves.

**Beat E — Routes**  
- Coastal vs. inland — both may matter; weights debated.

### Act III — Far horizons

**Beat F — Australia**  
- Early water crossings — remarkable seafaring implication (accessible wonder).  

**Beat G — The Americas**  
- Timing and routes (Beringia, coastal kelp highway ideas) — **Active debate** on earliest dates.  
- Present range of serious views without false certainty.

**Beat H — Everywhere humans go**  
- Ecological impact tease (megafauna later Ep. 27) — light foreshadow only.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Mini-arc close (20–25)**  
- Reptile age → extinction → mammals → early hominins → *Homo* bush → planetary *sapiens*.

**Beat J — Honesty beat**  
- Known: African origin framework; global dispersal; archaic encounters.  
- Unknown: exact pulse timings; route weights; some regional first-arrival dates.

**Beat K — Baton**  
- What made this expansion possible was not only legs — minds, language, culture…  
- Tease **The Evolution of the Human Mind**

## Do not claim

- Do not freeze contested first-arrival dates as settled.  
- Do not erase African diversity after “exit.”  
- Do not imply non-African peoples are less ancient in dignity — all living *sapiens* share deep African roots of the species.
