# S1E27 — The Ice Age

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Pleistocene glacial cycles reshape continents and life — megafauna rise and vanish; Neanderthals and *sapiens* adapt; the end of the last ice sets up farming worlds.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 27)  
**Comes from:** S1E26 (human mind)  
**Goes to:** S1E28 (prehistoric worlds after the ice)

---

## Learning goals

1. Explain **glacial cycles** as recurring climate swings, not one eternal freeze.  
2. Show **land bridges**, ice sheets, ecological upheaval.  
3. Present **megafauna** flourish-and-fade with human vs. climate debate.  
4. Place **Neanderthals and *sapiens*** in cold-world lifeways.  
5. End at the threshold of the **Holocene / farming window**.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The white edge

- Time-lapse ice sheets growing/shrinking; animals and camps moving.  
- Line: *The ice did not just cover the land. It rewrote the script of survival.*  
- Title: **The Ice Age**

### Act I — Climate machine

**Beat A — Pleistocene swings**  
- Glacials and interglacials; orbital forcings named lightly (Milankovitch) — **Established** framework.  

**Beat B — Geography changes**  
- Sea levels drop; Beringia and other bridges; coastlines migrate.  
- Maps that breathe.

**Beat C — Not endless winter everywhere**  
- Regional variety: steppes, refugia, tropics also change.

### Act II — People of the ice

**Beat D — Technology and society**  
- Clothing, shelters, hunting, fire, cooperation — mind from Ep. 26 applied.  

**Beat E — Neanderthals and *sapiens***  
- Overlap in cold Eurasia; cultural complexity; extinction context without caricature.  
- Callback Ep. 24.

### Act III — Giants and silence

**Beat F — Megafauna gallery**  
- Mammoths, giant sloths, etc. — wonder then fade in many regions.

**Beat G — Who killed the giants?**  
- Climate vs. human hunting vs. combination — **Active debate**.  
- Show both evidence types; no single Season 1 verdict forced.

**Beat H — End of the last glacial maximum**  
- Warming; ice retreat; new landscapes — agricultural windows opening (tease).

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: glacial cycles reshaped Pleistocene Earth; humans adapted; many megafauna extinctions clustered near end-Pleistocene.  
- Unknown: exact extinction cause mixes; regional cultural variation details.

**Beat J — Baton**  
- After the ice, a long prehistoric chapter of foragers and first farmers…  
- Tease **After the Ice: Prehistoric Worlds**

## Misconceptions to kill

| Myth | Correction |
|------|------------|
| One continuous ice age cartoon | Many cycles |
| Whole Earth frozen solid | Regional complexity |
| Humans alone OR climate alone always | Often debated combinations |
| Farming starts during peak ice | Mostly after warming windows |

## Do not claim

- Do not assert a universal single cause for all megafauna losses.  
- Do not portray Neanderthals as ice-age failures only.  
- Do not start Neolithic detail here — that’s Ep. 28.

---

## Honesty beat (closing card)

**Known:** Ice ages remade geography and tested humans; megafauna vanished in many regions near the end.  
**Unknown:** Full extinction recipes; every regional story.  
**Better evidence:** Tighter dated kill sites + climate proxies; ancient DNA of populations and fauna.
