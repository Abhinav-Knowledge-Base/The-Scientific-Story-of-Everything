# S1E12 — What Exactly Is Life?

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Before explaining origins, the series confronts a surprisingly hard question — what counts as alive — through cells, metabolism, reproduction, information, and evolution.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 12)  
**Comes from:** S1E11 (evidence of early life)  
**Goes to:** S1E13 (chemistry → life)

---

## Learning goals

1. List common **criteria** for life without treating any single checklist as sacred.  
2. Understand **edge cases** (viruses, sterile mules, fire, crystals, future AI teases lightly).  
3. Meet **cells**, **metabolism**, **replication**, **information (RNA/DNA)**, **evolution**.  
4. See why definitions matter for **origin-of-life** and **alien life** searches.  
5. Hand off a clear target: a Darwinian chemical system — without solving origins yet.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The failing checklist

- On-screen list: grows, eats, moves, reproduces…  
- Each item fails an edge case (fire “eats”; crystals “grow”; mule doesn’t reproduce…).  
- Line: *Life is obvious — until you try to define it.*  
- Title: **What Exactly Is Life?**

### Act I — Working pieces

**Beat A — Cells / compartments**  
- Boundary that keeps chemistry “inside.”  
- Cell as city metaphor (membrane walls).

**Beat B — Metabolism**  
- Energy flow and chemical transformation.  
- Not just “eating pizza” — gradients and reactions.

**Beat C — Reproduction**  
- Making more of the same kind — with caveats.

### Act II — Information and Darwinian evolution

**Beat D — Heredity**  
- RNA/DNA as information ribbons (visual).  
- Copying with possible change.

**Beat E — Evolution by natural selection**  
- Variation + inheritance + differential success.  
- Ladder: evolution of life on Earth = **Established**; as *the* definition of life = philosophical debate.

**Beat F — NASA-ish working definition (optional card)**  
- “Self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution” — useful, not holy.

### Act III — Edges and others

**Beat G — Viruses and gray zones**  
- Depend on hosts; force humility about borders.  
- **Active debate**: are viruses “alive”?

**Beat H — Alien humility**  
- Could life elsewhere fail our Earth checklist?  
- Link to Season 4 “are we alone?” without derailing.

**Beat I — Chemistry vs. biology boundary**  
- Gradient, not a magic spark line — prepares Ep. 13.

### Act IV — Baton

**Beat J — Honesty beat**  
- Known: useful scientific criteria; evolution as central hallmark of Earth life.  
- Unknown: a single necessary-and-sufficient definition everyone accepts.

**Beat K — Baton**  
- If life is a Darwinian chemical system, how did chemistry cross that threshold?  
- Tease **From Chemistry to Life**
