# Season 2 — The Deep Universe

**Role:** Depth season for the **natural world** — cosmology → stars/elements → Earth system → origin of life → evolutionary mechanisms and major transitions — in the same causal order as Season 1.  
**Episode count:** 26  
**Prerequisite:** Season 1 (recommended). Recap hooks at arc starts.

**Continuity rule:** Each arc deepens named Season 1 chapters; open questions planted in S1 are answered here at mechanism level (or marked still open).

**Arcs (timeline order):**
1. Cosmic stage & earliest universe — S1 Ep. 1–5 (Season 2 Ep. 1–6)
2. Stars, elements, galaxies — S1 Ep. 6–8 (Ep. 7–11)
3. Earth as a system — S1 Ep. 9–10, 15 climate stage (Ep. 12–16)
4. Origin of life — S1 Ep. 11–13 (Ep. 17–21)
5. Evolution & major transitions — S1 Ep. 14–22 (Ep. 22–26)

**Phase 3 detailed treatments:** [season-02/episodes/](season-02/episodes/README.md) — Episodes 1–26 complete.

**Detailed treatment:** [season-02/episodes/ep-01-space-time-and-gravity.md](season-02/episodes/ep-01-space-time-and-gravity.md)
