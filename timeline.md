# Master Cross-Season Timeline

Shared chronological reference across all four seasons of *The Story of Everything*. 

Exact ages and numerical dating represent the best current empirical consensus from radiometric dating, astrophysical models, and molecular clocks (subject to ongoing scientific refinement). 

The primary dramatic and pedagogical goal is **relative scale** and **unbroken causal continuity**: zooming from cosmic deep time ($10^9$ years) into geological eons ($10^6$ years), down into human prehistory ($10^4$ years), historic civilizations ($10^2$ years), and finally into the open horizons of deep time.

## 5. The Cosmic Calendar (The Pedagogical Metaphor)

If the entire 13.8-billion-year history of the cosmos is mapped onto a single 365-day calendar year:

```
    JANUARY 1, 00:00:00        The Big Bang (Cosmic Inflation & Light Elements)
    MAY 1                       The Milky Way galaxy forms its disk
    SEPTEMBER 1                 Formation of the Sun and the Solar System
    SEPTEMBER 21                First primitive single-celled life appears on Earth (Archean)
    NOVEMBER 9                  First complex eukaryotic cells emerge (Endosymbiosis)
    DECEMBER 14                 First multicellular animals appear (Ediacaran biota)
    DECEMBER 17                 The Cambrian Explosion of body plans
    DECEMBER 20                 First land plants colonize the continents
    DECEMBER 25                 Dinosaurs flourish across the Mesozoic
    DECEMBER 30, 06:24:00       Chicxulub asteroid impact wipes out non-avian dinosaurs
    DECEMBER 31, 20:10:00       Early hominins walk upright in the African Rift Valley
    DECEMBER 31, 23:48:00       Homo sapiens emerges in Africa
    DECEMBER 31, 23:59:32       Neolithic Agricultural Revolution begins
    DECEMBER 31, 23:59:48       Building of the Pyramids of Giza
    DECEMBER 31, 23:59:58       The Renaissance and the Scientific Revolution
    DECEMBER 31, 23:59:59       The Industrial Revolution, spaceflight, and digital networks!
```
