# S1E01 — Before the Beginning

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Before telling the birth of the universe, the series teaches what “beginning,” “space,” “time,” and “13.8 billion years” can mean in science — and where science must stay silent.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 1)

---

## Learning goals

By the end, viewers should be able to:

1. Distinguish **poetic** talk of “the beginning” from **scientific** models of the early universe.  
2. Explain the **observable universe** vs. “everything that exists.”  
3. Say what **~13.8 billion years** refers to (age since the hot dense early state we can model), without claiming a clock “before” time.  
4. Use the series **evidence ladder**: Established → Strong inference → Active debate → Speculation.  
5. Leave with the refrain: the story starts where **evidence** starts — and names what remains unknown.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The most dangerous sentence

- Black frame. A single line: *“In the beginning…”* (held).  
- Cut to: galaxies rushing, clocks melting into light, a human face looking up.  
- Narration challenge: Everyone thinks they know what that sentence means. Science does not use it the way poetry or scripture does.  
- Title card: **Before the Beginning**  
- Soft title under: *The Story of Everything*

### Act I — What are we even talking about?

**Beat A — Space is not an empty box**  
- Visual: room → remove walls → stars; then grid of space stretching.  
- Teach: In modern cosmology, space is part of the story — distances between things can grow.  
- Ladder: **Established** that space expands (preview; full case in Ep. 2).

**Beat B — Time is not a universal hallway**  
- Visual: two clocks in different gravity/speeds (gentle relativity nod, no equations).  
- Teach: “Before” only makes sense once time is defined by physical processes.  
- Near the earliest describable state, everyday “before/after” language can fail.

**Beat C — The observable universe**  
- Visual: sphere of light-travel distance around Earth; beyond it, darkness labeled “not nothing — not yet seeable / not in causal contact.”  
- Teach: We only have evidence from within our observable patch.  
- Key line: *The map is not the whole territory — but it is a real map.*

### Act II — What does “13.8 billion years” mean?

**Beat D — Age as a measurement**  
- Visual: master timeline unrolling from a glowing “t ≈ 0” mark — but the mark is labeled **edge of current models**, not “creation stamp.”  
- Teach: Roughly 13.8 billion years separates us from the hot, dense early state that Big Bang cosmology describes.  
- Compare scales: Earth ~4.5 Byr; human history as a blink (plant the cosmic-year motif lightly).

**Beat E — What we are *not* saying**  
- On-screen “NOT” cards:  
  - Not: a bomb in a preexisting warehouse of space.  
  - Not: a proven story of absolute nothing → something.  
  - Not: a complete theory of the first instant.

### Act III — Where science stops (and how it stays honest)

**Beat F — The wall of physics**  
- Visual: traveler walking a lit path that ends at fog labeled **quantum gravity / unknown**.  
- Teach: Our best theories (relativity + quantum) are not yet united; the earliest fraction of a second is where models break or become speculative.

**Beat G — Introduce the evidence ladder (series device)**  
- Full-screen graphic — this must be memorable; it returns every episode:

| Badge | Meaning | Cue line |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Established | Converging evidence | “Evidence shows…” |
| Strong inference | Best current explanation | “The leading inference is…” |
| Active debate | Serious disagreement | “Scientists still disagree…” |
| Speculation | Interesting, unproven | “This remains speculative…” |

- Mini examples from daily life (weather forecast vs. myth) then apply to cosmos.

**Beat H — Honesty beat**  
- What we know: there is a hot dense early universe we can describe back to extreme conditions; space expands; we can age the cosmos.  
- What we don’t: a final account of “before,” a complete theory of the first instant, why there is something rather than nothing *as a physics theorem*.

### Act IV — Worldview window (science first)

**Policy reminder:** Comparative, brief, neither advocacy nor attack. Science is not replaced.

**Beat I — Human questions that are older than telescopes**  
- Montage (respectful, non-caricature): creation hymns, philosophical “why anything?”, eternal-cosmos ideas, cyclical time ideas — labeled as **human meaning traditions**, not lab results.  
- Structure on screen:  
  1. **Scientific question:** What does evidence allow us to say about early cosmos?  
  2. **Human question:** Why is there something rather than nothing? / Did the cosmos begin?  
  3. **Overlap:** Both care about origins.  
  4. **Difference:** Science demands testable models; many traditions answer with meaning, revelation, or metaphysics.  
  5. **Open:** Viewers may hold both kinds of questions; the series will keep them labeled.

**Beat J — Series promise**  
- We will tell one continuous story from this edge of knowledge to stars, Earth, life, humans, civilization, and the present.  
- We will always show where evidence ends.  
- Refrain on screen: **How did we get here?**

### Tag — Baton

- Timeline marker pulses at the hot early universe.  
- Line: *If we cannot yet speak of “before,” we can still speak of what happened when the story becomes speakable.*  
- Smash to Episode 2 title tease: **The Big Bang**

## Evidence ladder (episode claims)

| Claim | Ladder |
|-------|--------|
| Space between galaxies grows (expansion) | Established (full evidence Ep. 2) |
| Observable universe is finite in light-travel reach | Established |
| Cosmos has a measurable age ~13.8 Byr in standard cosmology | Established (within model + data) |
| Everyday “time before the Big Bang” may be meaningless | Strong inference / conceptual |
| A complete physics of the first instant | Active debate + Speculation depending on proposal |
| Absolute creation from metaphysical nothing | Outside empirical science (label clearly) |

## Misconceptions to kill

| Misconception | Correction |
|---------------|------------|
| “Beginning” = bomb in empty space | Expansion of space; Ep. 2 deepens |
| Science has filmed time zero | Models + evidence have a reach; wall exists |
| Observable = all that exists | Observable is a horizon of evidence |
| Asking “what was before?” is stupid | It’s human; it may be ill-posed *in physics* |

## Do not claim

- Do not claim science has disproved all religious cosmologies (category error).  
- Do not claim science has proved a unique metaphysical creation story.  
- Do not mock traditions in the worldview window.  
- Do not present multiverse or “universe from nothing” popularizations as Established.

---

## Honesty beat (closing card)

**Known:** We inhabit an expanding cosmos with a measurable age in standard cosmology; evidence reaches a hot dense early state.  
**Unknown:** Ultimate origin; “before”; full theory of the first instant.  
**Better evidence would be:** A tested theory uniting gravity and quantum physics; sharper probes of the earliest universe (developed in later episodes).
