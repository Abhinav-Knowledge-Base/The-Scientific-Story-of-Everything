## Architectural Breakdown by Season

### [Season 1: The Complete Story (Episodes 1–35)](season-01/README.md)
* **The Cosmic Dawn (Ep. 1–8):** From the Big Bang through inflation, particles, atoms, stars, and galaxies.
* **The Young Earth (Ep. 9–10):** Solar system accretion, Hadean crust, and primordial oceans.
* **The Living Cell (Ep. 11–17):** Prebiotic chemistry, LUCA, the Great Oxidation Event, and eukaryotes.
* **Macroevolution (Ep. 18–22):** The Cambrian Explosion, terrestrialization, dinosaurs, mass extinctions, and mammals.
* **The Human Bush (Ep. 23–27):** Bipedal hominins, genus *Homo*, Out of Africa, language, and the Ice Age.
* **The Slowed Human Finale (Ep. 28–35):** Prehistoric foraging, agricultural cradles, the Axial Age, world faiths, calendars, science to 1940, the post-1940 connected world, and the open coda.

### [Season 2: The Deep Universe (Episodes 1–26)](season-02/README.md)
* **Cosmic Foundations (Ep. 1–6):** Relativity, cosmic expansion, cosmic inflation, baryogenesis, the CMB, and dark matter.
* **Stars & Galaxies (Ep. 7–11):** Population III stars, stellar lifecycles, nucleosynthesis, black holes, and the Milky Way.
* **Earth Systems (Ep. 12–16):** Habitable planets, the geodynamo, plate tectonics, atmospheric engines, and oxygenation.
* **Abiogenesis (Ep. 17–21):** Hydrothermal vents, the RNA World, protocells, proven experiments, and cosmic life demographics.
* **Evolutionary Mechanisms (Ep. 22–26):** Natural selection, population genetics, major evolutionary transitions, mass extinctions, and deep-time clocks.

### [Season 3: The Human Story (Episodes 1–30)](season-03/README.md)
* **Deep Human Evolution (Ep. 1–7):** Primate ecology, habitual bipedalism, stone tools, *Homo erectus*, archaic Neanderthals/Denisovans, African *Homo sapiens*, and planetary dispersals.
* **Mind, Language, & Culture (Ep. 8–12):** Social brain, recursive syntax, symbolic cave art, cumulative culture, and ice-age megafauna hunting.
* **Prehistory to Cradles (Ep. 13–17):** Forager networks, Neolithic domestication, monumental architecture (Göbekli Tepe), civilizational cradles, and writing/law/states.
* **Axial Thought, Faiths, & Time (Ep. 18–22):** The Axial Age, world religions as social architecture, global knowledge trade, timekeeping systems, and medieval scholarship.
* **The Scientific & Technological World (Ep. 23–26):** The Scientific Revolution, industrial fossil energy, Darwinian deep time, and early electrical computing networks.
* **The Planetary Species (Ep. 27–30):** Post-1945 international cooperation, media networks & the global village, molecular genetics & planetary risk, and the human synthesis.

### [Season 4: Frontiers (Episodes 1–15)](season-04/README.md)
* **Life Beyond Earth (Ep. 1–3):** Exoplanet demographics, solar system ocean worlds (Europa, Enceladus, Titan), and SETI biosignatures.
* **Minds (Ep. 4–6):** The Hard Problem of Consciousness, comparative animal minds, and synthetic intelligence/alignment.
* **Earth & Civilization Futures (Ep. 7–11):** Climate tipping points, biosphere rewilding, polycentric planetary governance, civilizational longevity, and the physics of space settlement.
* **Cosmic Destiny & Meaning (Ep. 12–14):** Deep Earth future (Pangaea Ultima, boiling oceans, red giant), the fate of the universe (Heat Death), and existential meaning.
* **Series Coda (Ep. 15):** *How Did We Get Here?* — The Grand Synthesis and the permanent invitation of science.
