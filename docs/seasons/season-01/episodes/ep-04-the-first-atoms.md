# S1E04 — The First Atoms

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Leftover matter builds light nuclei, then neutral atoms; the cosmic fog clears and the oldest light in the sky — the cosmic microwave background — is released.

**Phase 1 outline:** [season-01-the-complete-story.md](../../season-01-the-complete-story.md) (Episode 4)

---

## Learning goals

1. Explain **Big Bang nucleosynthesis (BBN)** in plain language: mostly hydrogen and helium.  
2. Explain **recombination**: electrons bind to nuclei; universe becomes transparent.  
3. Identify the **CMB** as that released light, still mapped today.  
4. See why the early cosmos was **chemically simple** — heavy elements wait for stars (Ep. 6+).  
5. Connect CMB **tiny ripples** to later structure (handoff to Ep. 5).

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The oldest light

- Pitch-black → microwave sky map blooms (Planck-style beauty, labeled as data visualization).  
- Line: *This is not a painting. It is light that has traveled since the universe was young.*  
- Title: **The First Atoms**

### Act I — Cooking the light nuclei

**Beat A — Minutes, not myths**  
- Clock: first **minutes** after the hot early state (order-of-magnitude storytelling).  
- Protons and neutrons forge light nuclei when cool enough for binding but still extreme.

**Beat B — The yield (Established)**  
- Periodic table: **H** and **He** light up almost alone; lithium faint.  
- Rough cosmic recipe: ~¾ hydrogen, ~¼ helium by mass (say “about,” not fake precision).  
- Ladder: BBN success matching observed abundances = **Established** pillar of Big Bang cosmology.

**Beat C — What BBN cannot make**  
- Carbon, oxygen, iron — dark on the table.  
- Line: *Life’s elements are not born here. They will need stars.*

### Act II — Waiting for atoms

**Beat D — Still a plasma**  
- For a long time after nuclei exist, the cosmos is a fog of charged particles; light scatters — opaque.

**Beat E — Recombination (~380,000 years)**  
- Electrons join nuclei → neutral atoms.  
- Photon mean free path skyrockets — light streams freely.  
- Name care: “recombination” is historical jargon (first combination); optional wry subtitle.

### Act III — The cosmic microwave background

**Beat F — What we see**  
- Free-streaming light stretches with expansion into microwaves — CMB.  
- Nearly uniform glow everywhere — **Established**.

**Beat G — Tiny ripples**  
- Amplify contrast: mottling at part-in-100,000 level.  
- These are seeds of future structure — **Established** that anisotropies exist and match growth of structure stories; detail of growth in Ep. 5.

**Beat H — Consilience callback**  
- Evidence pillars from Ep. 2 now emotionally complete: expansion + CMB + light elements.

### Act IV — Simple cosmos, honesty, baton

**Beat I — Chemical poverty**  
- Beautiful empty periodic table with only light elements.  
- Causal promise: Episode 6 will forge the rest.

**Beat J — Precision & puzzles (light touch)**  
- Lithium abundance tension / measurement debates = **Active debate** (one card, not a rabbit hole).  
- Other precision cosmology tensions previewed only if needed — Season 2 depth.

**Beat K — Honesty beat**  
- Known: BBN light nuclei; recombination; CMB mapped in detail.  
- Unknown: full small-scale early puzzles; exact path from ripples to first stars’ timing.

**Beat L — Baton**  
- Light is free — but no stars yet. A long dark age begins; gravity and dark matter work in secret…  
- Tease **The Cosmic Dark Ages**
