# S1E28 — After the Ice: Prehistoric Worlds

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** The overview slows — between melting ice and the first cities lies a long prehistoric era of foragers, early farmers, villages, megaliths, and the Neolithic transition that makes cradles earned, not sudden.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 28)  
**Comes from:** S1E27 (Ice Age ends)  
**Goes to:** S1E29 (cradles of civilization)

---

## Learning goals

1. Feel the **duration** of Holocene prehistory before cities.  
2. Sketch **forager diversity** without stereotypes.  
3. Present **agriculture** as multi-center and gradual.  
4. Include **villages, crafts, megaliths/ritual landscapes**.  
5. Hand off to urban cradles as a scaling step, not the start of humanity.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The long middle

- Master timeline: ice ends; huge bar before first cities; caption: *Most of Holocene humanity lived here.*  
- Line: *Civilization is late. Prehistory is the main human story by time.*  
- Title: **After the Ice: Prehistoric Worlds**

### Act I — A warming world

**Beat A — Environmental change**  
- Coastlines, forests, deserts shift; new resources and risks.  

**Beat B — Holocene stability (relative)**  
- More stable climate than glacial extremes — opens farming windows (not destiny).

### Act II — Ways of living

**Beat C — Forager worlds**  
- Seasonal rounds; landscape knowledge; mobility; myth-bust “primitive poverty” and “noble savage” alike.  

**Beat D — Domestication begins**  
- Plants and animals in multiple regions — Fertile Crescent and others.  
- Gradual, messy; **Strong inference** on processes; local sequences debated.

**Beat E — Why farm? / why not?**  
- Hypotheses: climate, population, culture, risk — **Active debate**.  
- Resistance and mixed economies.

### Act III — Settling without empires

**Beat F — Early villages**  
- Pottery, storage, denser living; specialists nascent.  

**Beat G — Megaliths and ritual landscapes**  
- Monuments without full urban states — complexity before cities.  
- Wonder + humility about meaning.

**Beat H — Health and inequality previews**  
- Farming costs: labor, disease, hierarchy seeds — not pure progress narrative.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: post-glacial adaptations; multi-center domestication; long village prehistory.  
- Unknown: complete adoption motives; full archaeological coverage; monument meanings.

**Beat J — Baton**  
- In a few places, villages scale into cities, states, and writing…  
- Tease **The Cradles of Civilization**

## Misconceptions to kill

| Myth | Correction |
|------|------------|
| History starts with cities | Deep prehistoric Holocene |
| Farming invented once | Multiple centers |
| Foragers = failed farmers | Successful strategies |
| Progress = instant improvement | Tradeoffs real |
