## Beat sheet

### Cold open — Authority and the sky

- Crystal spheres → telescope → elliptical orbits.  
- Line: *Evidence learned to overrule the old map of the heavens — carefully, contentiously, irreversibly.*  
- Title: **The Last Five Hundred Years**

### Act I — New astronomy and method

**Beat A — Copernicus (mid-1500s)**  
- Heliocentrism as proposal; not instant victory.  

**Beat B — Galileo**  
- Telescopic evidence; conflict with authority — historical nuance, not cartoon.  

**Beat C — Institutions**  
- Societies, journals, experiment + mathematics — evidence ladder’s ancestry.  

**Beat D — Newton**  
- Lawful cosmos synthesis — overview icon, not equation lecture.

### Act II — Life’s story rewritten

**Beat E — Deep time**  
- Geology stretches Earth’s age; biblical chronologies challenged as science — careful, non-mocking.  

**Beat F — Darwin**  
- Evolution by natural selection; hinge for Episodes 11–25’s meaning.  
- Public contested zones vs. scientific consensus — labeled honestly.

### Act III — Power and machines

**Beat G — Industry**  
- Fossil energy multiplies human power; cities; early environmental footprints.  

**Beat H — Early 20th century bridge**  
- Radio, early physics revolutions (glimpse), global wars’ threshold — **do not** steal Ep. 34’s UN/cooperation story.  
- Fuse lit: **1940**.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Meta beat**  
- This episode is why Season 1 can exist: instruments + method looking backward 13.8 Byr.

**Beat J — Honesty beat**  
- Known: scientific revolution remade knowledge; Darwinian evolution; industrial transformation.  
- Unknown/open: what “progress” means; science entangled with empire and war.

**Beat K — Baton**

- Tease **After 1940: A Connected World**
