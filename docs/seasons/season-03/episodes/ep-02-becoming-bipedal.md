## Beat sheet

**Cold open**  
Laetoli-style tracks: bipedal gait preserved in volcanic ash.

**Act I**  
Pelvis, feet, spine, and energetics—costs and benefits.

**Act II**  
Energy, carrying, heat, habitat: Active debate, not settled story.

**Act III**  
Traits arrive out of order; walking often precedes large brains.

**Act IV**  
Next: tools and fire expand what bodies can do.
