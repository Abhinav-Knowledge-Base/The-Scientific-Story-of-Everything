# S3E13 — Hunter-Gatherer Worlds

**Season:** 3 — The Human Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?* (human depth)  
**Logline:** Most of human history is foraging—diverse, skilled, ecological expertise, not a single stereotype.

**Phase 1 outline:** [../../season-03-the-human-story.md](../../season-03-the-human-story.md) (Episode 13)

**Goes to:** S3E14 Domestication and the Neolithic Transition

---

## Learning goals

1. Destroy the single caveman stereotype.
2. Show ethnographic and archaeological diversity carefully.
3. Highlight knowledge intensity of foraging lifeways.
4. Set up why farming is a trade-off, not pure progress.
5. Hand off to domestication.
