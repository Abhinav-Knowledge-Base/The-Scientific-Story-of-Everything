# Season 3 — The Human Story

**Role:** Depth season for **humanity** — from primates through mind, prehistory, cradles, Axial thought, faiths as social bonds, timekeeping, scientific age, and the post-1940 connected world — in historical order matching Season 1 Ep. 23–34.  
**Episode count:** 30  
**Prerequisite:** Season 1; Season 2 helpful for evolutionary mechanisms.

**Continuity rule:** Follows Season 1’s human timeline; slows where S1 slowed (prehistory → present) with nuance and comparative detail.

**Arcs (timeline order):**
1. Deep human evolution — S1 Ep. 23–25 (Season 3 Ep. 1–7)
2. Mind, culture, ice-age worlds — S1 Ep. 26–27 (Ep. 8–12)
3. Prehistory to cradles — S1 Ep. 28–29 (Ep. 13–17)
4. Axial Age, faiths, time — S1 Ep. 30–32 (Ep. 18–22)
5. Scientific age to ~1940 — S1 Ep. 33 (Ep. 23–26)
6. After 1940 & planetary humanity — S1 Ep. 34–35 (Ep. 27–30)

**Phase 3 detailed treatments:** [season-03/episodes/](season-03/episodes/README.md) — Episodes 1–30 complete.
