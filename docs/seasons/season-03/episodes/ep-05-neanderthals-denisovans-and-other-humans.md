## Learning goals

1. Present Neanderthal lifeways without caricature.
2. Show Denisovans mainly through DNA and sparse fossils.
3. Briefly acknowledge other archaic humans.
4. Treat admixture as Established where evidenced.
5. Frame extinction as loss and contingency, not destiny.
