# Master Timeline

Shared reference for all seasons. Exact ages are approximate and will be refined with scientific advisors in later phases. The dramatic point is **relative scale**, not memorizing every digit.

Use this timeline as a recurring on-screen motif: zoom from cosmic deep time into geological time, then into human time.

---

| Age (approx.) | Milestone | Season 1 anchor |
|---------------|-----------|-----------------|
| ~13.8 billion years ago | Earliest describable hot, dense state; expansion begins | Ep. 1–2 |

## Scale reminder (for narration / graphics)

If the full 13.8-billion-year story were compressed into a **single calendar year**:

- The Big Bang is January 1.
- The Solar System forms around early September.
- Complex animals appear in mid-December.
- Dinosaurs dominate mid-to-late December and vanish near December 26.
- *Homo sapiens* appear in the last hours of December 31.
- Recorded civilization fits in the last minutes.
- The industrial and digital age fits in the last seconds.

(Exact calendar mapping can be refined for a signature graphic; keep the pedagogical intent.)
