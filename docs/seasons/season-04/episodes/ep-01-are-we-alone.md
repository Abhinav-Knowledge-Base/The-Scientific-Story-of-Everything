## Learning goals

1. Frame the solitude question as empirical, not mystical.
2. Separate life, complex life, and detectable technology.
3. Introduce biosignature vs technosignature strategies.
4. Present the Fermi puzzle carefully, without slogan answers.
5. Hand off to the near hunt in the Solar System.
