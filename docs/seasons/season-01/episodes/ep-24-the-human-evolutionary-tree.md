# S1E24 — The Human Evolutionary Tree

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** A highly visual tour of genus *Homo* and relatives — migrations, interbreeding, and extinctions on a bushy tree; *Homo sapiens* is one surviving tip, not the destined peak of a ladder.

**Phase 1 outline:** [season-01-the-complete-story.md](../../season-01-the-complete-story.md) (Episode 24)  
**Comes from:** S1E23 (early hominins)  
**Goes to:** S1E25 (Out of Africa)

---

## Learning goals

1. See **multiple human kinds** sharing the Pleistocene.  
2. Meet major players at overview level: *habilis*, *erectus*, *heidelbergensis*, Neanderthals, Denisovans, *sapiens*, others.  
3. Treat **interbreeding** as established genetic fact where evidenced.  
4. Treat other humans’ extinction as **contingent loss**, not moral destiny.  
5. Hand off to *sapiens* dispersal in detail.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The family reunion that never was

- Overlapping silhouettes of different human forms on a shared landscape; tree graphic blooms behind them.  
- Line: *For most of human evolution, “human” was plural.*  
- Title: **The Human Evolutionary Tree**

### Act I — Genus *Homo* appears

**Beat A — What changes**  
- Larger brains (trend, not ladder), tools, wider ranging — with caveats and species variation.  

**Beat B — Early *Homo***  
- *H. habilis* and early forms — naming debates flagged (**Active debate**).  
- *H. erectus*: long-lasting, wide-ranging cosmopolitan chapter.

**Beat C — Tree not roster of ancestors**  
- Many dead ends; not every name is “our grandma.”

### Act II — Archaic humans

**Beat D — *Heidelbergensis* and kin**  
- Possible source populations for later lineages — relationships debated.

**Beat E — Neanderthals**  
- Europe/western Asia; sophisticated culture evidence; respectful reconstructions (anti-caricature).  

**Beat F — Denisovans**  
- Known largely from DNA and fragments — ghost populations in genomes.  
- Ladder: Denisovan admixture in some living people **Established**.

**Beat G — Other experiments**  
- e.g. *H. floresiensis* and others — brief, wonder + debate.

### Act III — Mixing and extinction

**Beat H — Interbreeding**  
- *Sapiens*–Neanderthal; *sapiens*–Denisovan DNA — **Established** in genomics.  
- Species boundaries blur in practice.

**Beat I — Extinction of other humans**  
- Contingency, climate, competition, absorption — **Active debate** on weights.  
- Emotional register: loss of human diversity, not triumph parade.

**Beat J — Only *sapiens* remains**  
- Open question: why? — planted for honesty, not fake answer.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat K — Honesty beat**  
- Known: bushy *Homo* tree; wide migrations; archaic DNA in living people.  
- Unknown: full taxonomy; complete contribution of archaic DNA; why sole survival of *sapiens*.

**Beat L — Baton**  
- Focus tightens on *Homo sapiens* leaving Africa…  
- Tease **Out of Africa**

## Evidence ladder

| Claim | Ladder |
|-------|--------|
| Multiple *Homo* species/forms existed | Established |
| *H. erectus* wide geographic range | Established |
| Neanderthal and Denisovan admixture with *sapiens* | Established (genomics) |
| Exact species names/boundaries | Active debate |
| Single cause of archaic extinctions | Active debate |
| Ladder of inevitable progress to us | False |

## Misconceptions to kill

| Myth | Correction |
|------|------------|
| Straight line of ancestors | Bushy tree |
| Neanderthals as dumb brutes | Evidence of complexity |
| No mixing between human groups | Genomics shows admixture |
| Other humans “had to” vanish for us | Contingency |

## Do not claim

- Do not draw a single agreed phylogeny as final.  
- Do not caricature archaic humans.  
- Do not celebrate extinctions as progress.

## Honesty beat (closing card)

**Known:** Many human forms; migrations; interbreeding evidenced in DNA.  
**Unknown:** Complete tree and extinction causes.  
**Better evidence:** More ancient genomes; new fossils; refined dates.
