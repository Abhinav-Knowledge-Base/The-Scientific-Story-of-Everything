## Beat sheet

### Cold open — Scale of trust

- Many small campfires (clans) → shared prayer/ritual calendar/court → one wider circle.  
- Line: *Beliefs about the ultimate also rewired who counted as “us.”*  
- Title: **Faiths, Values, and Human Bonds**

### Act I — Chronological overview (not devotionals)

**Beat A — After Axial**  
- Christianity rises and spreads; Islam in the 7th century; Buddhism expands; Hindu traditions develop; Judaism continues; other paths regional.  
- Expansion maps in **date order** — not a simultaneous dump.

**Beat B — Series discipline**  
- Fair, comparative; science segments elsewhere stay empirical; here is human social/religious history.  
- Neither advocacy nor attack card.

### Act II — Social architecture

**Beat C — Apart from (and beside) theology**  
- Ritual calendars, charity, marriage rules, dispute resolution, sacred literacy, identity beyond clan.  
- These are **functions** historians can describe — not proofs of metaphysical claims.

**Beat D — Scaling trust**  
- Shared sacred commitment can make large cooperation cheaper (in game-theory intuition, lightly).  
- Markets and states also scale trust — religion is one instrument among several.

### Act III — Cases and limits

**Beat E — Islam and Arabian tribal unification (one case)**  
- Pre-Islamic fragmented tribes → community (*ummah*), law, ritual life — wider cooperating society.  
- Present as **historical social pattern**, not missionary argument.

**Beat F — Parallels**  
- Buddhist networks; Christian congregations across ethnic lines; Confucian civic ethics — brief comparative panels.  

**Beat G — Limits**  
- Schism, holy war, coercion, exclusion — real.  
- When faith unites vs. divides — open question planted.

### Act IV — Bridge + baton

**Beat H — Medieval knowledge**  
- Learning centers along trade routes; transmission of math/astronomy/medicine — bridge toward science age (Ep. 33).  

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: faiths scaled communities; social functions documented; harms also documented.  
- Unknown: how to weigh bind vs. divide across cases; religion’s share vs. state/market in trust.

**Beat J — Baton**  
- Shared sacred calendars coordinated peoples — next, how humans keep time from sky to time zones…  
- Tease **How Humans Keep Time**
