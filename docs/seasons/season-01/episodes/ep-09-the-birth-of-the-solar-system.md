# S1E09 — The Birth of the Solar System

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** A collapsing nebula becomes the Sun and a family of unlike worlds — planets, asteroids, and comets — setting the stage for a violent young Earth.

**Phase 1 outline:** [season-01-the-complete-story.md](../../season-01-the-complete-story.md) (Episode 9)  
**Comes from:** S1E08 (Milky Way address)  
**Goes to:** S1E10 (young Earth)

---

## Learning goals

1. Tell the **solar nebula** story: collapse → spinning disk → star + planets.  
2. Explain why planets **differ** with distance (temperature/composition gradient — accessible).  
3. Distinguish **rocky planets, gas giants, ice giants**, and small bodies.  
4. See asteroids/comets as **leftovers and messengers**, not junk.  
5. Hand off to Earth’s formation amid chaos.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — Order from collapse

- Turbulent cloud → collapse → flattened protoplanetary disk with a glowing center.  
- Line: *Every world you know began as dust in a shared disk.*  
- Title: **The Birth of the Solar System**

### Act I — From cloud to Sun

**Beat A — Trigger and collapse**  
- Galactic setting (shock from nearby stars possible — **Strong inference** options).  
- Conservation of spin → disk (visual).

**Beat B — The Sun switches on**  
- Protostar → fusion ignition → young Sun.  
- Age marker: **~4.6 billion years ago** on master timeline.

**Beat C — Disk chemistry**  
- Inner disk hot; outer cold — ices survive farther out.  
- Seed of planetary diversity.

### Act II — Planet formation

**Beat D — Dust to planetesimals to planets**  
- Stickiness problem acknowledged lightly; growth through collisions.  
- Ladder: broad nebular disk model = **Established** framework; many micro-steps = active research.

**Beat E — The architecture**  
- Tour: Mercury→Mars rocky; Jupiter/Saturn gas giants; Uranus/Neptune ice giants (names + character).  
- Not a moral ranking.

**Beat F — Migration (careful)**  
- Giants may have moved; early Solar System was dynamic — **Active debate** on exact migration history (Grand Tack etc. as optional named speculation tier if used).

### Act III — Leftovers and difference

**Beat G — Asteroids and comets**  
- Belt and icy outer reservoirs; impacts deliver and destroy.  
- Why they matter for water/organics questions (handoff Ep. 10).

**Beat H — Why neighbors differ**  
- Same disk, different temperatures, timings, collisions — contingency.  
- Exoplanet diversity tease: our layout may not be typical — **Active debate**.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: age ~4.6 Byr; disk origin; planet classes; leftover small bodies.  
- Unknown: exact migration path; full water-delivery timeline; how typical we are.

**Beat J — Baton**  
- One rocky world in the inner disk will be smashed, remade, cooled, and flooded…  
- Tease **The Young Earth**
