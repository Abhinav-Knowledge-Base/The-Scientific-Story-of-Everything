# S1E32 — How Humans Keep Time

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** From sky clocks and sacred calendars to the civil year and today’s global time zones — roughly +1 hour east and −1 hour west of a shared meridian — the quiet infrastructure of a connected world.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 32)  
**Comes from:** S1E31 (faiths & coordination)  
**Goes to:** S1E33 (last five hundred years)

---

## Learning goals

1. Root timekeeping in **Sun, Moon, stars**.  
2. Survey **historic/religious calendars** comparatively.  
3. Explain civil **year / month / day** (Gregorian overview).  
4. Teach **time zones**: ~15° ≈ 1 hour; **+ east / − west** from UTC/prime meridian.  
5. Link synchronized time to **modern cooperation** (rail, telegraph, aviation, phones).

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — Many times at once

- Smartphone showing local time + world map; dissolve to stone observatory and festival moon.  
- Line: *You still live in more than one calendar — even if your phone hides it.*  
- Title: **How Humans Keep Time**

### Act I — Historic time

**Beat A — Sky clocks**  
- Day from rotation; month from Moon; year from seasons/Sun.  

**Beat B — Comparative calendars (overview gallery)**  
- Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Chinese, Mesoamerican (Long Count mention), Indic, Hebrew, Islamic *Hijri*, others.  
- Religious feast cycles; eras (regnal, prophetic/founder).  
- Fair, brief — not a conversion tutorial.

**Beat C — Why calendars are power**  
- Tax, ritual, agriculture, legitimacy — coordination tech.

### Act II — Civil time most viewers use

**Beat D — Gregorian framework**  
- Year, month, week, day — inherited reforms.  
- Politics of calendar reform — **Active debate**/history of resistance.

**Beat E — Plural time**  
- Civil + religious + seasonal still coexist in one life.

### Act III — The planet’s hour grid

**Beat F — Physics of zones**  
- Earth ~360° / 24 h ≈ **15° per hour**.  
- From prime meridian / **UTC**: zones generally **+ toward the east**, **− toward the west** (political zigzags acknowledged).  

**Beat G — Why it was invented**  
- Railways and telegraphs force synchronization; aviation and internet inherit it.  

**Beat H — Present connection**  
- Any city phone sits on that shared grid — “single village” soft infrastructure (callback Ep. 31 trust + forward Ep. 34).

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: astronomical bases; many calendar traditions; UTC zone system in global use.  
- Unknown: future standards (leap seconds etc.); how plural times will evolve.

**Beat J — Baton**  
- Coordinated time + inherited knowledge set the stage for the scientific revolution…  
- Tease **The Last Five Hundred Years**
