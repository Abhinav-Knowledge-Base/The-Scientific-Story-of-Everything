## Beat sheet

### Cold open — Merger

- One microbe engulfs another; instead of digestion, coexistence; mitochondria appear as former bacteria.  
- Line: *Complexity began with cooperation — or capture that became cooperation.*  
- Title: **The Rise of Complex Cells**

### Act I — The new cell type

**Beat A — Eukaryotic features (accessible)**  
- Nucleus; internal compartments; cytoskeleton; usually larger size.  
- Contrast with bacterial/archaeal simplicity of architecture (not “inferior”).

**Beat B — Timeline humility**  
- Eukaryotes appear long after first life and after oxygen’s rise begins — exact coupling **Active debate**.  
- Blurred band on master timeline.

### Act II — Endosymbiosis

**Beat C — Mitochondria**  
- Once free-living relatives of bacteria; now energy organelles with their own DNA remnants.  
- Ladder: endosymbiotic origin of mitochondria = **Established** (strong consensus).

**Beat D — Chloroplasts**  
- Later endosymbiosis with cyanobacteria-like partners → photosynthesis in plants/algae.  
- **Established** broad story; secondary endosymbioses exist (light touch).

**Beat E — How many events / exact sequence**  
- Details of host identity and steps: **Active debate**.  
- Don’t present one textbook cartoon as the only research view.

### Act III — Revolutionary consequences

**Beat F — Energy and complexity**  
- New energetic and organizational possibilities (keep non-dogmatic; avoid oversold “mitochondria explain everything” slogans).  
- Enables lineages that will build multicellular bodies.

**Beat G — Not a ladder to humans**  
- Most eukaryotes remain microbial; animals/plants are branches, not the point of the invention.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat H — Honesty beat**  
- Known: eukaryotes; mitochondrial/chloroplast endosymbiosis.  
- Unknown: full origin sequence; why when; all intermediate steps.

**Beat I — Baton**  
- Once cells can be complex, they can start living as teams…  
- Tease **Multicellular Life**
