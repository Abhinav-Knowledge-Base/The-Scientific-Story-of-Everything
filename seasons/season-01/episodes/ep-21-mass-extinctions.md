**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story

**Logline:** Repeated global die-offs collapse ecosystems and open evolutionary opportunity — ending with the end-Cretaceous impact that closes the age of non-avian dinosaurs and clears space for mammal rise.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 21)

**Goes to:** S1E22 (rise of mammals)

---

## Learning goals

1. Define a **mass extinction** as a geologically rapid, global diversity crash.  
2. Survey major events (the “Big Five”) at overview level.  
3. List cause types: **volcanism, climate, anoxia, impact**, cascading ecology.  
4. Hold extinction as both **tragedy and opportunity**.  
5. Spotlight **~66 Ma** end-Cretaceous as the hinge to the mammal chapter.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The crash

- Biodiversity curve climbing through Mesozoic wonder → vertical drop.  
- Silence over empty ecosystems.  
- Line: *Extinction is not a subplot. It is one of evolution’s main authors.*  
- Title: **Mass Extinctions**

### Act I — Definition and stakes

**Beat A — Normal vs. mass**  
- Background extinction always happens; mass extinctions are global pulses.  
- Selectivity: who dies is not random in simple ways — patterns matter (**Active debate** on details).

**Beat B — Creative destruction**  
- Niches empty; survivors radiate later.  
- Anti-teleology: opportunity ≠ destiny for humans.

### Act II — Tour of the Big Five (overview, not encyclopedia)

**Beat C — Flash cards**  
- End-Ordovician, late Devonian, end-Permian (the Great Dying), end-Triassic, end-Cretaceous.  
- Each: one cause hint + one image — volcanism, ocean anoxia, climate stress, etc.  
- Ladder: events **Established**; exact cause weights often **Active debate**.

**Beat D — End-Permian cautionary peak**  
- Closest to biosphere-wide catastrophe among the five — visceral scale without false precision.

### Act III — The dinosaur hinge (~66 Ma)

**Beat E — Impact**  
- Asteroid evidence: iridium, shocked quartz, crater — **Established** impact contribution.  
- Impact winter visual: dark, cold, collapsed food chains.

**Beat F — Not only a rock**  
- Deccan volcanism and other stresses may contribute — **Active debate** on relative weights.  
- Show both without false “either/or” war for Season 1.

**Beat G — Who dies / who lives**  
- Non-avian dinosaurs gone; birds persist; small mammals, crocs, turtles, etc. survive in various ways.  
- Contingency: different day, different survivors?

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat H — Honesty beat**  
- Known: repeated mass extinctions; end-Cretaceous impact strongly evidenced; survivors inherit emptied space.  
- Unknown: exact cause mixes for each event; full selectivity rules.

**Beat I — Baton**  
- In the emptied world, a long-overshadowed group gets its chance…  
- Tease **The Rise of Mammals**

## Evidence ladder

| Claim | Ladder |
|-------|--------|
| Multiple major mass extinctions in the Phanerozoic | Established |
| End-Cretaceous bolide impact | Established |
| Volcanism/climate often involved in extinctions | Strong inference / case-dependent |
| Exact % cause split for K–Pg | Active debate |
| Extinction “meant” to produce humans | False — do not claim |

## Misconceptions to kill

| Myth | Correction |
|------|------------|
| Only the dinosaur asteroid mattered | Several Big Five events |
| Impact alone is uncontested as sole K–Pg cause | Debate on contributing factors |
| Extinction is only sad ending | Also opens radiations |
| Dinosaurs “deserved” to lose | Contingency, not morals |

## Do not claim
