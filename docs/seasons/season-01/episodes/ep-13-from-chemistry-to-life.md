# S1E13 — From Chemistry to Life

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** The hardest gap on the map — how non-living chemistry became living systems — surveyed through competing hypotheses, real experiments, and clearly marked unknowns; plus a brief worldview window.

**Phase 1 outline:** [season-01-the-complete-story.md](../../season-01-the-complete-story.md) (Episode 13)  
**Comes from:** S1E12 (definition of life)  
**Goes to:** S1E14 (first cells / microbial world)

---

## Learning goals

1. State the problem: **abiogenesis** is unsolved.  
2. Map major research programs: **RNA world**, **metabolism-first**, **protocells/membranes**, settings (vents, surfaces, ponds).  
3. Separate **experimental achievements** from **full pathway claims**.  
4. Hold panspermia as partial-delivery option, not a full solution to “first life.”  
5. Host a short **worldview window** after the science map.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The cliff

- Left: hydrothermal chemistry / atmospheric sparks. Right: a living cell. A chasm labeled **not yet crossed in full**.  
- Line: *This is the most honest blank page in the story of everything.*  
- Title: **From Chemistry to Life**

### Act I — Stage and supplies

**Beat A — Prebiotic ingredients**  
- Water, organics, energy sources — callback to young Earth.  
- Exogenous delivery (meteorites) as supplement — **Established** that organics arrive; not that they solve origins alone.

**Beat B — Candidate theaters**  
- Hydrothermal vents  
- Surface ponds / wet–dry cycles  
- Atmospheric chemistry  
- Visual tour with “maybe” tags — **Active debate** which dominated.

### Act II — Hypothesis arena

**Beat C — RNA world**  
- RNA as catalyst + information — motivation and gaps.  
- Ladder: productive research program = Strong inference interest; “it happened exactly this way” = not Established.

**Beat D — Metabolism-first / other polymers**  
- Networks before genes; alternative genetic materials — brief, fair.

**Beat E — Protocells**  
- Membranes, compartments, coupling problem: info + energy + boundary.  
- Venn diagram of three requirements.

**Beat F — Combination likely**  
- Line: *Nature may have woven paths, not picked a single slogan.*

### Act III — Experiments vs. story completion

**Beat G — Landmark achievements (Established as lab facts)**  
- Amino acids / nucleotides under plausible conditions (classic and modern work — keep non-hagiographic).  
- RNA catalytic activity; vesicle growth — examples of progress.

**Beat H — The remaining canyon**  
- No complete, continuous demonstration of chemistry → evolving cells under early-Earth conditions.  
- Badge: **gap**.

**Beat I — Inevitability?**  
- Rare vs. likely — open; Season 2/4 returns.  
- Card: **Active debate / unknown**.

### Act IV — Worldview window + baton

**Beat J — Worldview window (science first already done)**  
- Comparative notes: creation narratives, emanation, eternal cycles, philosophical “life principle” ideas — **human meaning responses**, not lab substitutes.  
- Structure: agreement (wonder at life’s origin) / contradiction (testability) / open questions.  
- Neither advocacy nor attack.

**Beat K — Honesty beat**  
- Known: rich prebiotic chemistry possible; multiple serious hypotheses; important experimental milestones.  
- Unknown: the actual historical pathway; first Darwinian system.

**Beat L — Baton**  
- Somehow, cells appear in the record’s aftermath — bacteria and archaea remake the world…  
- Tease **The First Cells**

## Misconceptions to kill

| Myth | Correction |
|------|------------|
| Scientists already know exactly how life began | Unsolved |

| Miller–Urey “solved life” | Important milestone, not full pathway |
| Panspermia ends the question | Moves the problem unless first origin explained |

## Honesty beat (closing card)

**Known:** Plausible chemistry; serious hypotheses; real lab progress.  
**Unknown:** The historical route to the first living systems.  
**Better evidence:** Tighter early-Earth constraints; continuous experimental pathways; maybe alien second samples.
