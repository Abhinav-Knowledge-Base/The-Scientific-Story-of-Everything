# S1E15 — The Great Oxygenation Event

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Photosynthetic microbes oxygenate Earth — a planetary revolution that is catastrophe for some life and opportunity for others, recorded in rusting rocks and a changed sky.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 15)  
**Comes from:** S1E14 (microbial world)  
**Goes to:** S1E16 (complex eukaryotic cells)

---

## Learning goals

1. Explain **oxygenic photosynthesis** as biology producing free oxygen.  
2. Describe the **Great Oxidation Event (GOE)** as a drawn-out planetary transition, not a single afternoon.  
3. Read **geological evidence** (e.g. banded iron formations — accessible).  
4. Hold oxygen as both **toxin** and **energy opportunity**.  
5. Hand off to complex cells that flourish in a changed world.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — Rust and breath

- Gray-orange early Earth → continents rust; atmosphere shift visualized.  
- A modern breath in reverse time to a world without free O₂.  
- Line: *The air you breathe is a microbial invention — and a crime scene.*  
- Title: **The Great Oxygenation Event**

### Act I — The invention

**Beat A — Photosynthesis**  
- Light-powered chemistry; cyanobacteria as key players (and related histories carefully).  
- Waste product: oxygen.

**Beat B — Why it matters energetically**  
- Opens high-energy aerobic lifestyles later — seed for complexity.

### Act II — Not an instant flood

**Beat C — Oxygen sinks**  
- Early oxygen eaten by rocks, oceans, chemistry — delayed sky change.  
- “Whiffs” of oxygen before the main rise — **Active debate** on pattern.

**Beat D — Timing**  
- Roughly **~2.4 billion years ago** region for GOE (say “around,” show blurred band).  
- Ladder: broad GOE = **Established**; exact triggers/tempo = **Active debate**.

**Beat E — Geology vs. biology**  
- Both matter: biological production + geological sinks/tectonics.  
- Avoid single-cause slogan.

### Act III — Revolution

**Beat F — Banded iron and rock witnesses**  
- Visual: striped iron formations as oxygen meeting dissolved iron.  
- Other proxies mentioned lightly.

**Beat G — Crisis and opportunity**  
- Anaerobic worlds shrink — extinction/stress for many microbes.  
- New niches for those that tolerate or use O₂.  
- Possible climate side-effects (e.g. related glaciations) — **Active debate** on links; don’t overclaim.

**Beat H — Before/after biosphere maps**  
- Redox world redrawn.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: oxygenic photosynthesis changed Earth; GOE left geological scars; O₂ reshaped evolution’s possibilities.  
- Unknown: precise trigger mix; full tempo; number/meaning of early whiffs.

**Beat J — Mini-arc close (10–15)**  
- Habitable Earth → life evidence → definition → origin gap → cells → oxygen world.

**Beat K — Baton**  
- In an oxygen-richer world, a new kind of cell will merge partners into complexity…  
- Tease **The Rise of Complex Cells**
