# S1E02 — The Big Bang

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** The Big Bang is not an explosion in space — it is the expansion of space from a hot, dense early state, backed by converging evidence, with inflation and the first instant carefully labeled.

**Phase 1 outline:** [season-01-the-complete-story.md](../../season-01-the-complete-story.md) (Episode 2)

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The word that misleads

- Firework / bomb montage — then freeze. Red X: **This is not the Big Bang.**  
- Dough rising / grid expanding with raisins: galaxies don’t fly through space so much as space grows between them (metaphor limits acknowledged on-screen).  
- Title: **The Big Bang**

### Act I — What the theory says

**Beat A — Definition card (Established framework)**  
On-screen precise wording:

> Big Bang cosmology: in the past, the observable universe was hotter, denser, and smaller in scale factor; it has been expanding and cooling.  

**Beat B — Not a center, not an edge in the cartoon sense**  
- Visual: every point sees others receding (in large-scale average).  
- No preferred “middle of the explosion.”

**Beat C — Timeline hook**  
- Master timeline: from hot dense state → cooling → (later) atoms, stars, us.  
- Line: *Tonight is the opening chapter of that cooling story.*

### Act II — Why scientists trust the broad outline

**Beat D — Expansion / redshift (Established)**  
- Prism light from galaxies; stretch of wavelength.  
- Hubble–Lemaître idea in plain language: farther galaxies, faster recession (on average).  
- Modern surveys as beauty shots — data, not vibes.

**Beat E — Cosmic microwave background preview (Established)**  
- Full-sky afterglow; “fog cleared” tease for Episode 4.  
- Ladder badge: Established that a hot early photon bath exists and is mapped.

**Beat F — Light elements preview (Established)**  
- Periodic table with H and He glowing.  
- Early universe cooked light nuclei — detail in Ep. 4; here as third pillar.

**Beat G — Consilience card**  
- Three pillars lighting together.  
- Key line: *Different measurements, one story.*

### Act III — Inflation (handle with ladder)

**Beat H — Why inflation was proposed**  
- Horizon / flatness puzzles in accessible language (puzzles, not exams).  
- Inflation: a brief early epoch of extremely rapid expansion — **Strong inference / Active debate** on details.

**Beat I — What inflation would explain + what would confirm more**  
- Seed fluctuations → later structure (link to Ep. 5).  
- Primordial gravitational waves / better CMB polarization as future tests (no false certainty).

**Beat J — Speculation gate**  
- Multiverse talk (if mentioned at all): **Speculation** badge mandatory; one sentence max in Season 1.

### Act IV — Misconceptions, honesty, baton

**Beat K — Myth-bust table (rapid)**  

| Myth | Reality |
|------|---------|
| Explosion in empty space | Expansion of space |
| Bang had a center in the room | No special center in the standard picture |
| Science knows the first instant completely | Broad history yes; earliest slice incomplete |
| “Just a theory” = guess | In science, theory = tested framework |

**Beat L — Honesty beat**  
- Known: expansion, hot early universe, CMB, light-element success (preview).  
- Unknown: precise earliest physics; what (if anything) preceded the describable hot phase; inflation’s microphysics.

**Beat M — Baton**  
- As the universe expands and cools, energy becomes a soup of particles…  
- Tease **The First Seconds**
