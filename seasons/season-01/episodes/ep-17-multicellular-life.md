# S1E17 — Multicellular Life

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** Bodies are societies of cells — multicellularity evolves many times through specialization, cooperation, and the policing of cheaters; early forms are soft, strange, and incompletely fossilized.

**Phase 1 outline:** [Season 1 outline](../README.md) (Episode 17)  
**Comes from:** S1E16 (eukaryotes)  
**Goes to:** S1E18 (Cambrian diversification)

---

## Learning goals

1. State that **multicellularity evolved multiple times**.  
2. Explain **specialization** and division of labor.  
3. Introduce **cooperation vs. cheating** as an evolutionary tension.  
4. Treat early multicellular fossils with humility (Ediacaran soft bodies, etc.).  
5. Hand off to animal diversification without claiming inevitability.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The cheater

- A cooperating cluster; one cell defects and hogging resources; policing returns order — or the body fails.  
- Line: *A body is a peace treaty among cells.*  
- Title: **Multicellular Life**

### Act I — Many origins

**Beat A — Not a single invention**  
- Algae, animals, fungi, and others — independent experiments.  
- Anti-ladder: our lineage is one path.

**Beat B — Benefits**  
- Size, defense, specialization, new niches.  
- Costs: transport, cancer-like defection (accessible analogy).

### Act II — Rules of the society

**Beat C — Specialization**  
- Cells take roles; development as choreography (light evo-devo tease).

**Beat D — Conflict**  
- Cheater cells; cancer as modern echo of ancient tension (careful, not overclaim).  

**Beat E — Genetic toolkits**  
- Adhesion, signaling, developmental genes — **Strong inference** on importance; details **Active debate**.

### Act III — The early record

**Beat F — Soft and strange**  
- Ediacaran-style forms as atmospheric gallery — affinity debates labeled **Active debate**.  
- Preservation bias: soft bodies vanish easily.

**Beat G — Why the long wait?**  
- Complex cells exist long before obvious animals — open puzzle card.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat H — Honesty beat**  
- Known: multicellularity multiple origins; specialization real; early fossils incomplete.  
- Unknown: counts of true independent origins; full genetic enablement story; tempo to animals.

**Beat I — Baton**  
- When animals diversify in the seas, the fossil record suddenly looks explosive…  
- Tease **The Cambrian Explosion**

## Misconceptions to kill

| Myth | Correction |
|------|------------|
| Multicellularity happened once | Many times |
| More cells = automatic progress to humans | Contingent paths |
| Soft early life “didn’t exist” | Often just poorly preserved |
| Bodies have no internal conflict | Cheating is a real theme |
