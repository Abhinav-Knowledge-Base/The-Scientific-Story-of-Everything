# Series Bible (Lite) — Phase 1

Short operating rules for writers, producers, and partners. Expanded bible comes in Phase 4.

---

## Mission

Tell **one continuous scientific story** of how we got from the early universe to the world we inhabit — and to the questions we can now ask about what comes next.

Viewers should finish Season 1 with a durable mental map. Later seasons deepen chapters of that map without breaking continuity.

---

## Audience & tone

- **Curious general audience**, including people with little prior science background.
- **Grand but clear:** cinematic scale of imagery; precise, non-jargony language.
- **Intellectually honest:** wonder without hype; confidence without false certainty.
- Platform-agnostic (TV, digital, cinema, hybrid). Episode length is flexible; content depth is not.

---

## Continuity rules

1. Every episode answers a piece of: *How did we get here?*
2. Every episode ends by **handing the baton** to the next causal step (or, in deeper seasons, to the next layer of the same step).
3. Prefer **causal narrative** over topic encyclopedia: “because of X, Y became possible.”
4. Recurring **master timeline** motif appears throughout (see [timeline.md](timeline.md)).
5. Evolution and history are shown as **branching trees**, never as a ladder culminating inevitably in modern humans.

---

## Evidence ladder

Label claims (on screen or in scripts) using this ladder:

| Level | Meaning | On-screen cue (suggested) |
|-------|---------|---------------------------|
| **Established** | Strong, converging evidence; textbook-level consensus | “Evidence shows…” |
| **Strong inference** | Best current explanation from incomplete data | “The leading inference is…” |
| **Active debate** | Multiple serious scientific positions | “Scientists still disagree…” |
| **Speculation** | Interesting but unproven; clearly marked | “This remains speculative…” |

Never present speculation as established fact. Never hide debate to make the story feel cleaner.

---

## Honesty beat (required)

Near the end of every episode (or as a recurring mid-episode card), include:

- What we know and why
- What we do not know yet
- What would count as better evidence

Season 1 plants open questions; Seasons 2–4 return to them with depth.

---

## Worldview windows

At civilizational questions (origins of the cosmos, life, mind, purpose, death), the series may briefly compare how **philosophical and religious traditions** have approached the same human questions.

**Policy:**

1. Science segment first: evidence and models.
2. Then short, fair comparative notes (multiple traditions where relevant).
3. Then: points of apparent agreement, points of contradiction, open questions.
4. Never substitute religious claims for scientific evidence.
5. Never use the science segment as a vehicle to attack or promote religion.

Worldview windows are optional per episode; they are natural in Season 1 episodes 1, 11–13, 26, **30–32** (Axial Age; faiths as social bonds; historic and sacred time), 35, and throughout Season 4.

---

## Visual grammar

Primary tools (not talking-head lecture as the main mode):

- Animated **master timeline** (zoom in / zoom out)
- Evolutionary and genealogical **trees**
- Migration and geological **maps**
- Scale comparisons (atoms → planet → galaxy → observable universe)
- Reconstructions (early Earth, fossils, hominins, cities)
- Scientific diagrams (nucleosynthesis, DNA, plate tectonics)
- Time-lapse and process animations (stellar life cycles, oxygenation, ice ages)

**Recurring signature:** return to the shared timeline so viewers feel the difference between cosmic, geological, and human time.

---

## Naming & language

- Project name: *The Story of Everything*
- Refrain: *How Did We Get Here?*
- Documentation language: **English**
- Prefer precise everyday language; introduce technical terms once, then use them.

## Season roles

| Season | Role |
|--------|------|
| 1 — The Complete Story | The full map (35 episodes; slowed human finale) |
| 2 — The Deep Universe | Mechanisms of cosmos, Earth, life, evolution (26; same order as S1 nature chapters) |
| 3 — The Human Story | Human depth from primates through post-1940 (30; same order as S1 Ep. 23–34) |
| 4 — Frontiers | Unanswered questions, futures, meaning, coda (15) |
