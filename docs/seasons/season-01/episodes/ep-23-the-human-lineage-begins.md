# S1E23 — The Human Lineage Begins

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** From primates and apes to early African hominins — bipedal experiments and mosaic evolution on a branching tree, not a ladder climbing to modern humans.

**Phase 1 outline:** [season-01-the-complete-story.md](../../season-01-the-complete-story.md) (Episode 23)  
**Comes from:** S1E22 (mammal radiation)  
**Goes to:** S1E24 (Homo evolutionary tree)

---

## Learning goals

1. Place humans among **primates/apes** with shared ancestry.  
2. Kill **ladder thinking**; insist on a **branching tree**.  
3. Introduce **early hominins** and **australopithecines**.  
4. Explain **mosaic evolution**: walking, brains, tools on different schedules.  
5. Keep Africa as primary early stage without oversimplifying.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — Not a march

- Classic “march of progress” silhouette line — red X. Replace with branching bush.  
- Line: *We are not the tip of a ladder. We are one twig of a tree that had many human-like experiments.*  
- Title: **The Human Lineage Begins**

### Act I — Deep background

**Beat A — Primates**  
- Grasping hands, binocular vision, social complexity — ecological context.  

**Beat B — Apes and common ancestry**  
- Shared ancestry with chimpanzees/bonobos ~millions of years (range language, not fake precision war).  
- Ladder: common ancestry **Established**; exact last common ancestor anatomy incomplete.

**Beat C — What “hominin” means**  
- Lineage more closely related to us than to chimpanzees — working definition on screen.

### Act II — Standing up

**Beat D — Bipedalism early**  
- Often precedes big brains — key anti-ladder fact.  
- Trackways (Laetoli-style) as iconic evidence.

**Beat E — Why walk upright?**  
- Hypotheses: energy, carrying, thermoregulation, habitat — **Active debate**; no single proven slogan.

### Act III — Mosaic early hominins

**Beat F — Australopithecines and kin**  
- Small brains, upright walking, varied diets; multiple species.  
- Africa as main stage.

**Beat G — Mosaic board**  
- Traits fill unevenly: pelvis, teeth, hands, brain — different tempos.  

**Beat H — Many experiments**  
- Parallel and dead-end branches — contingency.

### Act IV — Honesty + baton

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: primate/ape context; early bipedal hominins in Africa; mosaic evolution.  
- Unknown: many species relationships; primary bipedal drivers; complete census of experiments.

**Beat J — Baton**  
- From these walkers, genus *Homo* will branch into a bush of human kinds…  
- Tease **The Human Evolutionary Tree**

## Misconceptions to kill

| Myth | Correction |
|------|------------|
| March of progress ladder | Branching tree |
| Big brains first | Walking often earlier |
| One ancestral line only | Many branches |
| Humans evolved from chimpanzees living today | Shared ancestor, not modern chimps |

## Honesty beat (closing card)

**Known:** Ape ancestry; early African bipedal hominins; mosaic change.  
**Unknown:** Full family tree of early forms; why bipedalism won where it did.  
**Better evidence:** New fossils; better dates; refined phylogenies.
