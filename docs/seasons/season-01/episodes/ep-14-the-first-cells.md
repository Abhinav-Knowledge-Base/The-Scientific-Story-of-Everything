# S1E14 — The First Cells

**Season:** 1 — The Complete Story  
**Series question:** *How did we get here?*  
**Logline:** After the origin gap, cellular life — bacteria and archaea — diversifies, shares genes sideways, and engineers ecosystems long before animals exist.

**Phase 1 outline:** [season-01-the-complete-story.md](../../season-01-the-complete-story.md) (Episode 14)  
**Comes from:** S1E13 (origin hypotheses)  
**Goes to:** S1E15 (Great Oxygenation)

---

## Learning goals

1. Identify **bacteria** and **archaea** as deep domains of cellular life.  
2. Explain **horizontal gene transfer (HGT)** as complicating a simple tree.  
3. See early life as **ecology**, not isolated cells.  
4. Understand microbes as **planetary engineers**.  
5. Hand off to photosynthesis/oxygen as the next planetary plot twist.

## Beat sheet

### Cold open — The real Earthlings

- Animal silhouettes fade; microbial world blooms in soil, ocean, rock.  
- Line: *If aliens studied Earth fairly, they might call it the Planet of Microbes.*  
- Title: **The First Cells**

### Act I — Domains

**Beat A — What a cell already is**  
- Callback Ep. 12: membrane, metabolism, heredity — now assumed present.

**Beat B — Bacteria and archaea**  
- Two vast groups; archaea not “ancient bacteria” cartoon.  
- Diversity of metabolisms: eat rock, breathe strange chemicals (accessible wonder).

**Beat C — LUCA (careful)**  
- Last universal common ancestor as a **research construct** — not a photographed Adam microbe.  
- Ladder: **Strong inference** that cellular life shares deep homology; details of LUCA **Active debate**.

### Act II — Inheritance sideways

**Beat D — Vertical tree vs. horizontal links**  
- Classic tree animation; then arrows between branches — HGT.  
- Especially rampant in microbes.

**Beat E — Why it matters**  
- Early evolution may look more like a web.  
- Antibiotic resistance as modern intuition pump (brief, clear).

### Act III — Ecosystems

**Beat F — Mats and consortia**  
- Microbial mats layer by metabolism; waste of one feeds another.  
- Cross-section cathedral visual.

**Beat G — Engineering environments**  
- Local chemistry rewritten; preview of planetary oxygen without paying off fully.  
- Biofilms on early shores.

**Beat H — Lost chapters**  
- Soft bodies; geological recycling — much early history gone.  
- Honesty about incompleteness.

### Act IV — Baton

**Beat I — Honesty beat**  
- Known: microbial cellular life ancient and diverse; HGT real; ecosystems predate animals by eons.  
- Unknown: deepest branching order details; timing of many metabolic inventions.

**Beat J — Baton**  
- Among microbial inventions, one metabolism will leak a gas that remakes the sky…  
- Tease **The Great Oxygenation Event**
