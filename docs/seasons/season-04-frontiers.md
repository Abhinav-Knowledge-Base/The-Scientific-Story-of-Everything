# Season 4 — Frontiers

**Role:** Depth season for **unfinished questions** — life elsewhere, minds, planetary futures, cooperation’s next chapter, cosmic fate, and meaning — then a series coda that returns to Season 1’s refrain.  
**Episode count:** 15  
**Prerequisite:** Season 1; Seasons 2–3 recommended.

**Continuity rule:** Picks up open questions from S1 Ep. 35 and mechanism/human threads from S2–S3; stays timeline-aware (near future → deep future → cosmic end → meaning).

**Arcs:**
1. Life beyond Earth — S1 Ep. 35; S2 Ep. 21 (Season 4 Ep. 1–3)
2. Minds — S1 Ep. 26, 35; S3 mind arc (Ep. 4–6)
3. Earth & civilization futures — S1 Ep. 34–35; S3 Ep. 27–29 (Ep. 7–11)
4. Cosmic destiny & meaning — S1 Ep. 35; S2 Ep. 5 (Ep. 12–14)
5. Series coda (Ep. 15)

**Phase 3 detailed treatments:** [season-04/episodes/](season-04/episodes/README.md) — Episodes 1–15 complete.
